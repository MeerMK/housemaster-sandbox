What a human player sees

Bot accounts look exactly like regular players: a nickname, a stack, and normal timing. There's nothing on the table that marks them.

Turn on Reveal bots to see which seats are run from the dashboard. When several of them sit at one table, they're controlled by one person. That lets them share information and gang up on the humans without anyone noticing.
